Softopen Businesses that are about to open, before they pick their vendors.

Every day, restaurants, bars, shops, salons and gyms file for liquor licenses, health inspections, sales tax permits and business licenses weeks before they open. Softopen reads those filings, scores each one for whether it's a real independent storefront, and serves them as leads to sales reps and to the AI agents that prospect for them. Any lead can be confirmed by a person who phones the business.

For agents

GET  /v1/leads?state=TX&industry=Restaurants%20and%20food&min_score=75
GET  /v1/leads/{id}
POST /v1/leads/{id}/confirm        {"questions": ["Who picks the POS?"]}
GET  /v1/confirmations/{id}
