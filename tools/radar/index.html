Stay on the right side of the rules

  • CAN-SPAM (email): use an honest From name and subject line, include your valid physical postal address, and give a clear way to opt out. Honor opt-outs within 10 business days. The default email templates include your address and an opt-out line, so fill in Setup first.
  • TCPA (calls and texts): no autodialed or prerecorded calls and no automated texts to cell phones without prior express written consent. Dial one at a time, by hand, to business lines. A number found in public records is not consent to text.
  • No AI voices: the FCC ruled in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA, so never use AI voice cold calls. Cold texting also carries state rules, for example Texas SB 140 telemarketing registration.
  • Cash discount, not surcharge: some states, including Connecticut and Massachusetts, restrict surcharging. Present the program as a discount from the posted card price, with clear signage, and follow card network rules.
  • Do not call lists: check the National Do Not Call Registry and your state list before cold calling, and keep your own internal list.
  • Respect do not contact: if anyone asks you to stop, flag the lead as Do not contact. Radar hides it by default, disables copy and email buttons, and keeps the flag in exports and backups.
  • Public records are not permission: keep outreach one-to-one, relevant, and business to business. Do not resell the data. This is general guidance, not legal advice.
